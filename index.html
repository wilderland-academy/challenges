    How it works

    1. Pick

      Choose a challenge and tell your Field Guide your plan.

    2. Practice

      Use the “Where to learn” links on each challenge, and ask for help.

    3. Show it

      Show your guide in person, at Test Day, or with proof from someone outside school.

    4. Pass, or not yet

      No partial credit and no limit on retries. A not yet tells you exactly what to practice.

    5. Rank up

      Every pass is a credit. Credits move you up your trail.

    Four words to know

    Challenge
    One “I can” statement with a clear standard.
    Credit
    One passed challenge from your grade band or higher.
    Epic
    A bigger build over several weeks, with 3 checkpoints. Worth 2 credits.
    Gate
    A safety check you pass before an activity, like your fire card before you light a fire.

    Everyone starts at the bottom of their own trail, whatever their age. You receive each rank at Honor Court, with your feat named out loud. The passport means one challenge each with a real audience, a night outdoors, teaching someone, leading a group, a hard physical feat, and building with tech. Rank names are working names; the Founders Crew picks the final ones.

    About “not yet”

    You will hear it a lot. So does everyone who gets good at anything. A not yet comes with what was missing, how to practice it, and a retry date. Before a hard test your heart will race. That’s your body sending oxygen to your brain and muscles. Use it.

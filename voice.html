Voice feelings practice

Ten short clips with no faces. Every voice says the same plain words, so the only clue is how the voice sounds. Listen, then pick the feeling.

    How to get good at this

    1. Listen to each clip more than once. Notice how fast, how loud, and how high or low the voice is.
    2. In practice mode, when you miss one, play it again and listen for what gives it away.
    3. Do a few sets on different days. The clips change every time.
    4. When you score 8 or more twice in a row, ask your guide for the real check.

    Guides: switch to Test mode for the check. It hides every answer until the end, then shows the key. Start a fresh set so the clips are new to the student. The pass standard for FE-01.2 is 8 of 10.

    Voice clips: The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS) by Steven R. Livingstone and Frank A. Russo, PLoS ONE, 2018, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
